# Surnia support

[العربية](support-ar)

Surnia runs open-source language models entirely on your iPhone and iPad. There is no account and nothing to sign in to.

## Common questions

**How do I start?** Tap "Get a model" in the chat, or tap the model button at the top and choose "Manage models", then download a model that is marked "Suitable" for your device. Qwen3.5 0.8B is the smallest.

**How do I send a photo?** Use a model with the "Vision" tag (Qwen3.5, Gemma 4 or Ministral 3). Its vision add-on downloads together with the model. For a model installed without the add-on, attach a photo and tap Download in the row above the composer. Then tap + → Photo Library or Take Photo.

**How do I change the language?** Settings → App language. The app switches immediately. The same setting is in iOS Settings → Surnia → Language.

**A reply was wrong or offensive.** Tap the "…" button under the reply (or long-press it) and choose "Report a response". This opens an email to us with the reply and your device's details included. If no mail app is set up, write to the address below.

**Where is my data?** Only on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy) ([العربية](privacy-policy-ar)).
